Privacy

Effective August 14, 2026.

The workbench runs in your browser. Review briefs, claims, source descriptions, findings, and decisions are saved in browser local storage and are not submitted to RN Collins, an AI model, or a database by this site.

You control exported Markdown, CSV, JSON, and print/PDF files. Clearing browser site data or using reset removes the saved browser copy.

Do not enter privileged, confidential, restricted, sealed, export-controlled, personally identifying, or otherwise sensitive information. Use sanitized descriptions.

Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary technical request information for delivery, security, and operational logging. External LinkedIn and portfolio links follow those services’ practices. Any manual engagement will use a separate written data-handling agreement.
