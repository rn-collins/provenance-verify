Open Methodology

The EVIDENCE Framework

The framework underlying every Source Integrity Audit. Published openly because a standard only matters if others can evaluate it, adopt it, and hold it accountable.

E — Express vs. Implied Claims

Explicit assertions ("X causes Y") carry a different evidentiary burden than implied ones ("X may support Y"). Most AI-generated content conflates the two. Each claim is categorized before verification begins.

V — Verifiability Grade

Direct (specific source retrievable), Indirect (vague attribution — "studies show"), or None (no source anchor). Only direct sources can be verified. The others can only be noted as unverified.

I — Integrity of Source Type

Primary (peer-reviewed original research, court filings, regulatory text) outranks secondary (journalism, summaries, explainers). Tertiary sources (Wikipedia, AI-generated summaries) are flagged for what they are.

D — Dating & Currency

A source can be real and retrievable and still be wrong because the field moved. Currency requirements vary by domain — a 2018 regulatory citation is different from a 2018 neuroscience citation. Each is assessed against its field's pace of change.

E — Explicitness of Attribution

"Research shows" is not attribution. A named author, journal, year, and findable DOI or URL is. Vague quantifiers ("many," "most," "experts say") are catalogued and flagged, not silently accepted.

N — Neutrality & Conflict Disclosure

Who funded the study? Does the citing source have a financial relationship with the claim? Sponsor-funded research is not disqualified — it is disclosed, because the reader's judgment requires the information.

C — Confidence Level

Strong (multiple independent primary sources, consistent findings, current), Moderate-Test (limited evidence, single study, or mixed results — publishable with caveats), Exploratory (early-stage, theoretical, or anecdotal — use only with explicit framing).

E — Evidence Grade Summary

A — Fully verified, primary, current, explicit attribution. B — Verified with minor documentation gaps. C — Partially traceable. D — Low traceability, claims require qualification. F — Fabricated, retracted, or unverifiable by any means.
